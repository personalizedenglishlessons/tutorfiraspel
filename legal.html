Your data is stored on Supabase, a trusted cloud hosting service protected by Row-Level Security (RLS). The Supabase servers holding your data are located in Japan (Tokyo), meaning your personal data is transferred to and stored outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, which we disclose here as required by the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). We transfer your data to this provider only to run the platform (sign-in, your lessons, your progress), never for advertising or marketing purposes. Any communication over WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We also store your preferences (dark/light theme, language, and sign-in session) locally on your device using Local Storage, and never share them with anyone.
